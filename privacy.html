Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how Feenix Concepts (“we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use the Feenix Property Manager app, the web app and this website. We are based in Nigeria and follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in protected form and are not readable by us.
  • Records you enter: properties, units, tenants (such as names and phone numbers), rent and other payments, receipts, maintenance records and your business details, including logo and signature.
  • Subscription details: your email, plan, status and payment dates. Card and bank details are handled by Paystack. We do not see or store them.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app, keep your records in your account and sync them across your devices.
  • To send account emails such as sign-up verification and password reset.
  • To manage your subscription and trial.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Tenant and property data

The tenant and property information you enter belongs to you. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to record it. We store it only to provide the service to you.

4. Reminders and WhatsApp

Renewal reminders are scheduled on your device. When you tap a reminder, the app opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. We do not read your WhatsApp messages.

5. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the service: Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (website hosting), Paystack (payments) and Brevo (account emails). They process data only to provide those services.

6. Storage and security

Each account’s data is kept separate and is protected with access controls. Your data is also stored on your device. You can download a backup at any time. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and cloud data, email us at the address below. Backups you downloaded stay on your own device.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it is used. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

The service is meant for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the service means you accept the updated policy.
